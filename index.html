Jeremy Murphy & Associates are delighted to present to the market this exceptional residential site, ideally located within walking distance of Rosscarbery village and the beautiful Warren Beach.

Conveniently located just a short distance from Rosscarbery village, the property offers an excellent opportunity to create a wonderful family home or holiday residence in one of West Cork's most sought-after coastal locations.

The site benefits from outline planning permission with no restrictions

LOCATION

This charming property enjoys an enviable location, combining the tranquillity of a coastal setting with excellent connectivity to key destinations. It is situated approximately 12 km from Clonakilty, 60 km from Cork Airport and Cork City, and approximately 45 km from Kinsale, making it an ideal base for those seeking peaceful countryside living while remaining within easy reach of the region's main towns and amenities.

The property is also within a pleasant 10-minute walk of Rosscarbery Village. Rosscarbery is a vibrant and welcoming village offering a variety of cafés, restaurants, pubs and local amenities, making it an attractive location for both families and holidaymakers.

For those who enjoy outdoor pursuits, the nearby Rosscarbery Lagoon Activity Centre offers a range of water sports and outdoor activities, while Smugglers Cove Adventure Centre provides a fantastic selection of activities for children and visitors of all ages.

Rosscarbery also boasts tennis courts, a pitch-and-putt course and excellent coastal walking routes, further enhancing the area's appeal as a lifestyle destination.

Adding to the convenience of this superb location, the Celtic Ross Hotel & Leisure Centre is just a short walk away. With its combination of a peaceful rural setting, proximity to the coast and village amenities, and excellent transport links, this site represents an exciting opportunity to build a home in a truly desirable West Cork location.
